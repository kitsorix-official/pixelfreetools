Pixel Art FAQ

Every Pixel Art Question, Answered

Short, straight answers to the questions people actually search - from "is it hard?" to "can I sell it?".

The Basics

What is pixel art?

What is pixel art?

+
A form of digital art where images are built pixel by pixel on a grid, with every square placed deliberately. It grew out of the low-resolution graphics of early arcade games and 8-bit consoles.

Is pixel art drawing?

+
Yes - it is drawing, but with pixels as the mark instead of freehand strokes. Every pixel is placed intentionally, like a tiny square brush stroke.

Is pixel art 2D?

+
Yes. Classic pixel art is a flat, 2D medium. The 3D equivalent is voxel art, which uses cubes instead of pixels.

Is pixel art 8 bit?

+
Not always. 8-bit describes the NES and Game Boy era look, but pixel art spans 16-bit SNES/Genesis, GBA and modern indie styles like Celeste and Stardew Valley.

Is pixel art retro?

+
The style is strongly tied to retro games, but it is still actively used today. Calling it retro refers to the nostalgic 8-bit and 16-bit aesthetic.

Is pixel art real art?

+
Yes. Pixel art is a recognized art form with its own history, communities and professionals, spanning games, animation and illustration.

Is pixel art a game?

+
No. Pixel art is an art style, not a genre. Many games simply use it for their visuals.

Are sprites pixel art?

+
Sprites are game images, and many are made as pixel art. But not all sprites are pixel art, and pixel art is also used for icons, tiles, backgrounds and illustrations.

Who invented pixel art?

+
The word "pixel" was first published in 1965, and the term "pixel art" appeared in 1982 from Adele Goldberg and Robert Flegal of Xerox PARC. The art form itself grew from arcade games like Space Invaders (1978) and Pac-Man (1980).

Why is pixel art so popular?

+
Nostalgia for retro games, a low barrier to entry, fast production, and the charming constraint of limited resolution keep it popular with hobbyists and indie developers alike.

Are pixel art games still popular?

+
Yes. It remains one of the most popular styles in indie games - Celeste, Stardew Valley, Undertale and many more ship with it every year. Learn more about pixel art.
Difficulty & Learning

Is pixel art hard?

Is pixel art hard?

+
The basics are easy - most people draw a first sprite in minutes. Mastering outlines, shading and animation takes practice, but it is one of the most approachable digital art styles.

Is pixel art easier than drawing?

+
For most beginners, yes. The grid and small canvas are forgiving, and you place squares instead of freehand strokes. Traditional skills still help, but they are not required to start.

Is pixel art easy?

+
Getting started is easy - a simple 16x16 sprite can be finished in a few minutes. Making complex, polished art is where the skill comes in.

Is pixel art hard to learn?

+
No. Because it uses a small, structured grid, you can pick up the fundamentals in days and improve steadily with practice.

Is pixel art fun?

+
Most people find it relaxing and rewarding - a bit like a puzzle. The immediate, visible result of each placed pixel keeps it satisfying.

Should I learn pixel art?

+
If you enjoy retro aesthetics, want to make your own game assets, or want a low-cost art hobby, absolutely. It has a gentle learning curve and great communities. See how to start.
Tools & Software

Where and how can I make pixel art?

Which software is best for pixel art?

+
Aseprite (~$20 once) is the industry standard. Great free options are Pixelorama, LibreSprite, Piskel and Pixilart. See the full comparison.

Where can I create pixel art?

+
In a browser with Piskel, Pixilart or Pixelorama; on desktop with Aseprite or LibreSprite; or on mobile with Pixel Studio or Pixelc.

Can I make pixel art in Procreate?

+
Yes. Use a small canvas, turn off stream line, pick a 1px hard pencil with no anti-aliasing, and zoom in to place pixels on the grid.

Can I make pixel art in Photoshop?

+
Yes. Use a small canvas, a hard round 1px brush with anti-aliasing off, and set image resampling to "Nearest Neighbor" so scaling stays crisp.

Can I make pixel art in Krita?

+
Yes. Krita ships with dedicated pixel art tools and a grid view, making it a strong free option.

Can I make pixel art in GIMP?

+
Yes. Zoom in, use a 1px pencil rather than a soft brush, and disable smoothing. GIMP also supports indexed color palettes.

Can I make pixel art in Clip Studio Paint?

+
Yes. Clip Studio includes pixel and grid-friendly tools, and many artists use it for retro style work.

Can I make pixel art in Godot?

+
Yes. Godot has a built-in pixel art editor and import settings that keep pixel art crisp when scaled. Many developers also make pixel art games with Godot.

Can I make pixel art in Blender?

+
Possible but not ideal. Blender is a 3D tool; its texture painting and Grease Pencil modes can do pixel work, but dedicated editors are far more practical.

Can I make pixel art in Canva?

+
Canva is limited for pixel art. Its AI "PixelArt Studio" is not a real pixel editor, so use a dedicated tool for hand-made sprites.

Can I do pixel art in MS Paint?

+
Yes - MS Paint is the classic choice. Zoom in, enable the grid, and use the 1px pencil tool.

Can I draw pixel art with a mouse?

+
Yes. Because you place individual pixels rather than freehand strokes, a mouse works perfectly. Many professional artists started with a mouse.

Is pixel art free? Does it cost money?

+
You can make pixel art completely free with Piskel, Pixilart, Pixelorama or LibreSprite. Paid tools like Aseprite (~$20 once) add power but are optional.

Does pixel art need wifi or internet?

+
No. Desktop editors like Aseprite, Pixelorama and LibreSprite work fully offline. Only browser or cloud-based apps require a connection.

Does pixel art have ads or dark mode?

+
The art form itself has neither. Some free mobile apps (Pixilart, Pixel Studio) show ads and charge for a paid ad-free tier; dark theme is sometimes part of that tier too.
Sizes & Specs

What size should pixel art be?

What size should pixel art be?

+
Common sprite and tile sizes are 16x16, 32x32, 48x48 and 64x64. The best size depends on how much detail you need. See the full size guide.

How big should pixel art sprites be?

+
For games, 16x16 to 64x64 covers most tiles and characters; 64x64 leaves room for detail. Whole-screen targets like NES 256x240 or SNES 256x224 are also common.

Should pixel art have outlines?

+
It is a style choice, not a rule. Outlines make characters readable against busy backgrounds; many artists use them, but clean styles work fine without.
3D, Vector & AI

Pixel art and new technology

Is pixel art 3D?

+
Classic pixel art is flat and 2D. The 3D version of the look is voxel art, made with tools like MagicaVoxel and Qubicle.

Can pixel art be vector?

+
You can convert pixel art to vector shapes, but it stops being pixel art - curves replace the individual squares. Pixel art is inherently raster (grid-based).

Does pixel art use AI?

+
Hand-made pixel art does not. AI pixel art generators do, and they are useful for concepts and placeholders - but generic generators usually produce "pixel-styled" images that still need manual cleanup.

Which AI is best for pixel art?

+
Dedicated tools that generate on a fixed grid - Sprite AI, Retro Diffusion and PixelLab - beat general models like Midjourney or DALL-E for game-ready pixel art.
Selling & Copyright

Can I sell pixel art?

Can I sell pixel art?

+
Yes. Sell original pixel art on itch.io, Fiverr, Etsy, Gumroad and game asset stores. See the selling guide.

Can pixel art be copyrighted?

+
Yes. Original pixel art is automatically protected by copyright the moment it is created. Selling or reusing someone else's sprites - even lightly edited - without permission is not allowed.
Games & Everything Else

The rest

Is Undertale pixel art?

+
Yes. Undertale (2015, by Toby Fox) uses a retro pixel art style and renders at 640x480, with sprites designed for crisp integer scaling.

What is pixel art in Minecraft?

+
People build pixel art in Minecraft by placing blocks on a grid. You can convert an image into a block-by-block map with tools like Spritecraft.

How to do pixel art in r/place?

+
r/place is a massive collaborative pixel canvas. During events, communities coordinate to place individual pixels over time to build shared art.

Is pixel art safe?

+
Making pixel art is a safe hobby. Just check the privacy terms of any online editor or mobile app before uploading your work.

Is pixel art haram?

+
There is no single universal ruling - it depends on what is depicted and which scholarly interpretation you follow. Depicting inanimate objects is generally considered acceptable, while depictions of living beings are debated. Ask a scholar you trust for guidance.

Do pixel art tattoos age well?

+
Like all tattoos, they blur over time. Bold outlines and larger pixel blocks age noticeably better than tiny, fine details.

How do I make pixel art?

+
Pick a free tool, use a small canvas (16x16 or 32x32), turn the grid on, and start placing pixels. Begin with simple shapes, then learn outlines and shading. Read the beginner guide.
